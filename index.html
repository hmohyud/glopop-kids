Peptides

Amino acid chains
What it is
Short chains of amino acids, the building blocks of protein. The pack lists them simply as peptides: which ones, and from what source, is TBD.
In your body
You digest peptides like any food protein, into smaller pieces and amino acids. Some small peptide fragments have even been measured in the blood after people ate them.2,3
The research, honestly
The studies cited here are on collagen peptides, and their findings are still debated.4,5 Why peptides are in a kids pop: TBD.

Vitamin C

Vitamin
What it is
An essential vitamin your body can't make, so it has to come from food.7
In your body
Vitamin C contributes to the normal function of the immune system, to normal energy-yielding metabolism and to the protection of cells from oxidative stress. Your body also needs it to build stable collagen.8,9
The research, honestly
Those are general wordings about vitamin C. The amount in a pop is TBD, so they are not a promise about the pop. And vitamin C isn't a sunscreen.10

Vitamin B12

Vitamin
What it is
The only vitamin with a metal at its center: cobalt. Hence its other name, cobalamin.12,13
In your body
B12 contributes to normal energy-yielding metabolism, normal red blood cell formation and the normal functioning of the nervous system.9,12
The research, honestly
If you already get enough B12, more won't give you extra energy.12

Vitamin B8 (biotin)

Vitamin
What it is
A water-soluble B vitamin.14 The pack calls it vitamin B8; most people just call it biotin.
In your body
It's a helper for five enzymes that work on fats, carbs and protein. Biotin contributes to normal energy-yielding metabolism.9,14
The research, honestly
There's little good evidence that extra biotin does anything more if you already get enough. High doses can also throw off some lab tests, so tell the doctor before your child has a blood test.14
  • Energy metabolism
  • Enzyme helper

Fun fact. Raw egg white holds a protein, avidin, that grabs biotin and keeps you from absorbing it. Cooking the egg switches it off.14

Hyaluronic acid

Chain molecule
What it is
A long, slippery chain molecule that your body makes itself.15
In your body
It's known for holding on to water. And the body is busy with it: an adult recycles about a third of their hyaluronic acid every day.15,16
The research, honestly
The research on eating hyaluronic acid comes mostly from small trials and is still debated.17 Our amount is TBD. Why it is in a kids pop: TBD.

Zinc

Mineral
What it is
An essential trace mineral. Your body has no zinc storage, so a steady daily supply matters.19
In your body
Zinc contributes to the normal function of the immune system, to the protection of cells from oxidative stress and to normal protein synthesis. Nearly 100 enzymes need it to work.9,19
The research, honestly
The zinc in mineral sunscreen is a different form doing a different job. Eating zinc doesn't protect you from the sun.20

        Sun sense

        Sunny day? Sunscreen first.

        Plain sun sense for sunny days. A pop is not sunscreen.

        • Is it sunscreen?

          No. Nothing you eat replaces sunscreen, and vitamin C isn't a sunscreen either.10 Dermatologists recommend broad-spectrum, water-resistant SPF 30 or higher, reapplied about every two hours and after swimming or sweating.38

        • Beach-bag rules

          Keep pops in the shade, out of direct sun and hot cars, and out of reach of little ones. Skip the fridge: condensation makes hard candy sticky. Keep the bag sealed and dry.25

          General hard-candy tips. Storage directions on the label: TBD.

        • Savor, don't crunch

          Biting hard candy can chip teeth. Savor it, sip some water after, and brush as usual.39 Hard candy is also a choking hazard for young children, so keep pops out of reach of little ones.
